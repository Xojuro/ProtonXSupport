About

Once enabled, ProtonX monitors your server's audit log in real time. If a non-whitelisted user performs a dangerous action, their roles are stripped and they're immediately kicked, then the action they took gets reverted.

Setup requirement: Make sure the ProtonX role is placed at the very top of your role hierarchy, above all other roles. Give ProtonX all permissions, Administrator is recommended, so it can properly monitor, punish, and revert any nuke attempts without restriction.

Detected actions: creating/deleting channels or roles, granting dangerous permissions, banning/kicking members, changing the server name or icon, and creating webhooks. Per-permission whitelisting is also available for trusted mods.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ProtonX?

ProtonX is a Discord security and anti-raid/anti-nuke bot designed to help protect your server from malicious actions and unauthorized moderation activity, along with many multipurpose commands.

ProtonX can detect actions such as:

  • Creating or deleting channels
  • Creating or deleting roles
  • Granting dangerous permissions
  • Banning or kicking members
  • Changing the server name or icon
  • Creating webhooks
  • Adding bots
Is ProtonX a powerful anti-nuke bot?

We do not consider ProtonX to be the most powerful anti-nuke system. However, it is capable of detecting, stopping, and removing users who attempt to raid or nuke your server.

ProtonX is designed to provide an additional layer of protection and help minimize damage caused by malicious actions.

How do I set up ProtonX correctly?

First of all, use !xanti-on

Setup requirement:

  • Make sure the ProtonX role is placed at the very top of your role hierarchy, above all other roles.
  • Give ProtonX all necessary permissions. Giving all permissions is recommended, so it can properly monitor, punish, and revert nuke attempts without unnecessary restrictions.

If ProtonX does not have sufficient permissions or is placed below certain roles, it may not be able to properly take action against users with higher roles.

Can I whitelist trusted moderators?

Yes. Use !xwhitelist for per-permission whitelisting, allowing you to give trusted moderators access to specific actions without triggering the protection system.

Once a user is whitelisted, they can bypass the configured protection for those actions.

Use !xantibot-on to ensure that users with full access cannot bypass protection for adding bots or creating webhooks. These actions will remain restricted exclusively to the server owner for additional security.

Use !xantibot-off to disable this restriction.

How can I view all ProtonX commands?

To view the complete list of commands and available features, use: !xhelp
