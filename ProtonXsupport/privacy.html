Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy explains what data ProtonX collects and how it is used.

1. Data we store

  • Server IDs and per-server settings (anti-nuke, anti-link, anti-spam, prefix, auto role, log channels, join-to-create setup, premium status, embed drafts, per-server avatar and banner).
  • User IDs for whitelisted and bypassed users, and AFK status (reason and time).

2. Data we process

  • Message content is read to run anti-link, anti-spam, and commands. It is not stored by anti-link or anti-spam.
  • If a server owner enables message, voice, member, or profile logs, the Bot posts those events (including deleted and edited message text) in the channel they choose.
  • Audit log entries are read to detect who performed dangerous actions.

3. Operator logs

To keep the Bot running and prevent abuse, the Bot operators receive logs in a private channel: command usage (user, server, and the command message), servers added or removed, whitelist changes, anti-nuke triggers, and errors.

4. How we use data

Only to provide protection and features, fix bugs, and prevent abuse. We do not sell your data.

5. Sharing

Data is hosted on our infrastructure providers and shared only as required by law. Anything sent through Discord is also subject to Discord's own policies.

6. Retention and deletion

Server settings are kept while the Bot is in your server. To request deletion of your server's or your own data, contact us on our support server with your server or user ID.

7. Children

The Bot follows Discord's minimum age requirements and is not intended for children under 13.

8. Contact

Questions? Join our support server. See also our Terms of Service.
